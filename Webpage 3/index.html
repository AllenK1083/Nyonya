About Nyonya

Comforting, vibrant Malaysian cooking.

Nyonya is a down-to-earth Malaysian restaurant in New York City’s Chinatown, serving classic dishes built around aromatic herbs, coconut, chilies, noodles, rice, seafood, and rich curry sauces.

The menu includes vegan-friendly options, and the restaurant is cash-only. For dietary needs or ingredient questions, call before visiting so the team can help guide your order.
